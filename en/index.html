FAQ

Q. Can I move my records to another device?
A. Records are saved only on your device, so they can't be transferred. Deleting the app also deletes your records.

Q. I can't find my friend in co-op.
A. Open the app on both devices in the same place, with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth turned on. If you didn't allow "Local Network" access when asked, go to the iPhone Settings app → this app → turn on "Local Network".

Q. I watched an ad but couldn't revive.
A. You can't revive if the ad was closed before it finished, or if it couldn't load because of the connection. Please try again somewhere with a good connection.

Q. How do I change how my data is used for ads?
A. For tracking, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In US states with privacy laws, you can also tap "Privacy settings" on the title screen to opt out of the sale or sharing of your data.
